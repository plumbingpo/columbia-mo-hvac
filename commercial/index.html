Food Service Comfort

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

Hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort all interact, and a system that's undersized or failing shows up first as a sweltering kitchen and complaints at the tables. Cooking lines pour huge heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air out as fast as the rooftop units can make it. We handle rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Comfort and air balance are critical issues for any food-service operator. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with written service records that show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. We provide detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

Replacement and maintenance plans are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits the hours you're open. If a job can't be done during service, we install during off-hours, keeping the kitchen running and guests comfortable in the meantime.

Safe Installation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. Our technicians inspect heat exchangers for cracks, confirm proper venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: protecting the people in your building from CO hazards, poor air quality, and water damage. Findings go into a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

Regular professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and acting on expert recommendations keeps minor issues from becoming liabilities. It also builds a paper trail. We keep service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or auditor asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Columbia

Each hour without climate control costs your business money. For a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. In a retail store, it sends shoppers home on a summer afternoon. Columbia HVAC offers fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Columbia and surrounding areas, dispatching an experienced technician quickly when you call.
